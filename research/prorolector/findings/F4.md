## Summary Assessment

ProRector is a niche, Uzbekistan-origin school timetable software with 17 years of history and 3,500+ claimed installations. User testimonials (all from the official website) are overwhelmingly positive, emphasizing dramatic time savings (3 days → 2-3 hours) and ease of use. However, the software has virtually zero independent review presence — no Capterra, G2, Trustpilot, or Reddit discussions exist. It does not appear in major software comparison articles. The $12/year price point is remarkably low, and the social media promotion-for-free-license program suggests active growth efforts. All testimonial data is self-published by the vendor, so independent verification is limited. The software appears to be primarily a desktop application with no cloud/web/mobile version.
