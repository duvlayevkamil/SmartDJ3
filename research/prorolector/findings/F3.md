| Lesson scheduling must consider: maximum daily hours, break times, ventilation breaks | Research analysis | 2026-07-11 | MEDIUM |
| Elementary school weekly load analysis shows hygienic requirements | https://www.sapub.org/global/showpaperpdf.aspx?doi=10.5923/j.ajmms.20251504.27 | 2025-04-08 | MEDIUM |

### Typical SanPIN Requirements for Timetable Software
Based on research, SanPIN-compliant timetable software should consider:
1. **Maximum daily lesson hours by age group** (varies by grade level)
2. **Mandatory break periods** between lessons
3. **Ventilation breaks** (5-10 minutes after consecutive lessons)
4. **Lesson duration standards** (typically 40-45 minutes for elementary, 45 minutes for secondary)
5. **Physical education placement** (not in first/last slots, after ventilation breaks)
6. **Heavy subjects placement** (math, science in morning slots)
7. **Maximum weekly hours** by grade level

### ProRector and SanPIN
- ProRector appears to have some built-in conflict detection that could support SanPIN compliance
- No explicit "SanPIN mode" or automated SanPIN validation was found in the software
- Users would need to manually configure constraints to match SanPIN requirements

## 6. Market Analysis Summary

### Competitive Landscape

| Solution | Type | Primary Use | Market Position |
|----------|------|-------------|-----------------|
| ProRector | Standalone desktop app | Timetable generation | Dominant in standalone timetable |
| Kundalik/eMaktab.uz | Web platform (government) | Full school management | Official government platform |
| MyMaktab | Web CRM | School management | Growing, 100+ schools |
| Maktab AI | AI platform | Teacher productivity | Emerging |
| e-timetable.uz | Web platform | Timetable management | Competing with ProRector |

### Key Findings

1. **ProRector dominates** the standalone timetable software market with 3,500+ institutions
2. **Kundalik/eMaktab.uz** is the official government platform with timetable functionality
3. **ASUTP** was not found as a specific named product - likely a functional description
4. **Multiple local solutions** exist, indicating a competitive market
5. **SanPIN compliance** is important but not automated in most solutions
6. **Language support** is critical - ProRector's 33+ languages give it an advantage
7. **Mobile access** is increasingly important (Kundalik has mobile apps)

### Recommendations for Competitors
- SanPIN automated compliance checking would be a differentiator
- Integration with Kundalik/eMaktab.uz ecosystem could be valuable
- Uzbek-language interface and support is essential
- Affordable pricing is important for the market (ProRector is noted as "affordable")
